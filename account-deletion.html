Request Submitted

Your account deletion request has been received. Our support team will review your request and contact you within 5–7 business days for identity verification.

Before You Delete

Consider the following before submitting your deletion request:

  • Ensure all pending transfers are completed or cancelled
  • Withdraw any remaining balance from your account
  • Download any receipts or records you wish to keep
  • Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone

What Gets Deleted

Upon successful account deletion, the following data will be permanently removed:

  • Your account profile and personal information
  • Saved recipient details
  • Transaction history (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Payment methods and linked accounts
  • App preferences and settings

Data Retention

Some information may be retained as required by law or for legitimate business purposes:

  • Transaction records may be retained for regulatory compliance (SECP, BNM requirements)
  • Anti-fraud and security logs may be retained for a limited period
  • Aggregated, anonymized data may be retained for analytics purposes
