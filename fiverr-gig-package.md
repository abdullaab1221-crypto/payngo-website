Q: Do you build custom WordPress themes or use pre-made templates?
A: I do both depending on what fits your project. For simpler sites, I use well-coded starter themes or Elementor. For projects that need something unique, I build custom WordPress themes from scratch with clean, optimized code.

Q: Will my website be mobile-responsive?
A: Yes. Every website I build is fully responsive and works perfectly on phones, tablets, and desktops. Mobile-first design is standard in all my projects.

Q: Do you include SEO setup?
A: Yes. I set up proper meta titles, descriptions, heading structure, and schema markup in every project. For advanced SEO needs, I configure Rank Math or Yoast plugin.

Q: Can I update the website content myself after delivery?
A: Yes. WordPress makes it easy to edit pages, add blog posts, and update content. I will guide you on how to manage your site after delivery.

Q: Do you provide hosting and domain?
A: I can recommend hosting providers and help you set up, but hosting and domain costs are separate from the project price.

Q: What if I need changes after the project is delivered?
A: Every package includes revisions during the project. The Premium package also includes 30 days of post-delivery support. For ongoing support, we can discuss a maintenance arrangement.

Q: Can you redesign my existing WordPress website?
A: Yes. I can redesign your existing site to give it a modern, professional look while keeping your content and SEO intact.

Q: Do you build WooCommerce stores?
A: Yes. I can set up WooCommerce with product listings, cart, checkout, and payment integration. This is included in the Premium package.

Q: What do I need to provide before you start?
A: I will need your content (text, images, logo), any reference websites you like, your brand colors/preferences, and details about the features you need.

Q: How do you handle WordPress login for delivery?
A: For delivery, I recommend creating a temporary WordPress admin account with limited access. I will show you how to set this up safely. Never share your main admin credentials.
