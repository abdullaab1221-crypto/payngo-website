The Little Things That Made Us

The moments that didn't make the timeline — but made our love

Moment 1
01

The Last Cinnamon Latte

She ordered the last cinnamon latte. He walked in, soaked, and the barista pointed at her table. What could have been a quick apology became a four-hour conversation. We still can't smell cinnamon without smiling.

Moment 2
02

Kitchen Dances

Somewhere between the frying pan and the sink, we started dancing to whatever song was playing. No lessons, no plans — just two people swaying in a tiny kitchen, laughing until the music ended and beyond.

Moment 3
03

Reading the Same Book

We decided to read the same book at the same time — one chapter a night, out loud. It took us a year to finish. It's still the best story we've ever shared, mostly because of the pauses in between.

Moment 4
04

The Promise Tree

On our fifth anniversary we planted a young tree in our garden. We water it, watch it, and argue over whose turn it is. We made a promise: if it grows, we grow — roots together, always reaching toward the light.
