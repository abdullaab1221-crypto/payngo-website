Chapter One

Where It All Began

Mia was sitting by the window of a small cafe in Karachi, working on a story she could never quite finish. Alex walked in, drenched, ordered the last cinnamon latte — and her heart decided the story was worth rewriting.

They talked about everything except the obvious: that neither of them wanted the evening to end. The cafe closed at eleven; they left at midnight, still talking under the rain.

That week, Alex found every excuse to pass by that cafe. By spring, the window table had become theirs.

Chapter Two

Growing Together

Love, we learned, is not one moment — it is a thousand small ones. Sharing umbrellas, learning each other's favourite songs, fighting over the last slice of pizza, and making up before the pizza got cold.

We grew in the same direction, never apart. Mia's stories started getting published. Alex started his own little business. And every single milestone, we celebrated together — because what is success if not shared?
