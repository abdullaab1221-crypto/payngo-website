The First Spark

How it all began — the moment two hearts found each other and a beautiful journey started.

❤️ 2010
Where It All Began

The First Meeting

It was a crisp autumn evening in 2010 when Wei and Lin met at a mutual friend's dinner party. What began as polite introductions quickly evolved into hours of conversation about dreams, cultures, and the kind of life they both envisioned.

They discovered a shared passion for travel, photography, and cooking. By the end of the evening, they both knew this was something special — a connection that would stand the test of time.

🌟 2013
Building Our Foundation

A Home of Our Own

After three years of dating, Wei and Lin moved into their first apartment together. It was a small space, but they filled it with love, laughter, and dreams for the future.

They adopted a cat named Mochi, started a small herb garden on the balcony, and spent weekends exploring hidden gems in their city. Life was simple, but perfect.

👶 2016
A Family Grows

Welcome to the World

In 2016, their family grew with the arrival of little Mei. The apartment that was once filled with two people now echoed with the sounds of a baby's laughter and the pitter-patter of tiny feet.

Two years later, Kai joined the family, completing their beautiful circle. Life had never been fuller, busier, or more meaningful.

🌴 Today
Our Life Today

The Journey Continues

Today, the Chen family lives in a cozy house with a big backyard. Mei loves painting, Kai is obsessed with dinosaurs, and Mochi still rules the house.

Every day brings new adventures, new memories, and new reasons to be grateful. This is our story — and it's still being written.
