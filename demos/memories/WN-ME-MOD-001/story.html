How It All Began

The beautiful story of how our family came to be — from the first spark of love to the life we've built together.

❤️ 2012
Chapter 01

The First Spark

It was a warm evening in June 2012 when Maria and Carlos first met at a mutual friend's gathering. What began as a casual conversation quickly turned into hours of shared stories and laughter.

They discovered a shared love for travel, food, and adventure. By the end of the night, they both knew this was the beginning of something extraordinary.

🌟 2015
Chapter 02

Building Our Dream

After three years of dating, Maria and Carlos decided to take the next step. They moved into their first apartment together — a cozy space that quickly became filled with love and laughter.

They adopted a golden retriever named Coco, started a vegetable garden, and began planning for the family they dreamed of creating.

👶 2017
Chapter 03

A Family Grows

In 2017, their family grew with the arrival of little Sofia. The house that was once filled with just two people now echoed with the sounds of a baby's laughter.

Two years later, Lucas joined the family, completing their beautiful circle. Life had never been fuller or more meaningful.
