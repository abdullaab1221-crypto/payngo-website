Frequently Asked Questions

Home / FAQ

Orders

How do I place an order?
Browse our collection, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. You can pay via credit/debit card, bank transfer, or cash on delivery. You'll receive an order confirmation email immediately after placing your order.
Can I modify or cancel my order?
We process orders quickly. If you need to modify or cancel, contact us within 2 hours of placing the order. After that, we may not be able to make changes as the order may already be in processing.
How do I track my order?
Once your order ships, you'll receive an email and SMS with a tracking number. You can also log in to My Account and view your order status in the Orders section.
What if I receive the wrong item?
We apologize for the error. Contact us immediately with your order number and a photo of the item received. We'll arrange a free return and ship the correct item to you right away.

Shipping

How long does shipping take?
Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days. Express shipping delivers in 1-2 business days. Same-day delivery is available in Lahore, Karachi, and Islamabad for orders placed before 2 PM.
Is shipping free?
Yes! Standard shipping is free on all orders over Rs. 5,000. For orders under Rs. 5,000, a flat rate of Rs. 300 applies. Express and same-day delivery have additional charges.
Do you ship internationally?
Yes, we ship to over 50 countries worldwide. International shipping rates and delivery times vary by destination. You can see the estimated cost at checkout.
What if my package is lost?
Contact us and we'll investigate with our shipping partner. If the package is confirmed lost, we'll either reship your order or provide a full refund.

Returns & Exchanges

What is your return policy?
We offer 30-day returns on all items in their original condition with tags attached. Earrings, intimate apparel, and final sale items cannot be returned. Visit our Returns page for full details.
How do I return an item?
Log in to My Account, select the order, and click "Request Return." We'll provide a prepaid shipping label. Pack the item and drop it off at your nearest courier location.
When will I receive my refund?
Refunds to credit/debit cards take 5-7 business days. Store credit is issued within 1-2 business days. Bank transfers take 3-5 business days. You'll receive an email when your refund is processed.
Can I exchange for a different size?
Absolutely! We offer free size exchanges. Simply request an exchange through My Account or contact us on WhatsApp. We'll ship the new size as soon as we receive your return.

Payment

What payment methods do you accept?
We accept Visa, Mastercard, bank transfers, JazzCash, EasyPaisa, and cash on delivery. All online payments are processed through secure, encrypted channels.
Is it safe to pay online?
Yes, absolutely. We use industry-standard SSL encryption and our payment gateway is PCI DSS compliant. We never store your card details on our servers.
Do you offer installment plans?
We're working on introducing installment payment options. For now, you can pay in full or choose cash on delivery where available.

Account

Do I need an account to shop?
No, you can checkout as a guest. However, creating an account gives you benefits like order tracking, wishlist, saved addresses, and faster checkout.
How do I reset my password?
Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and enter your email. You'll receive a password reset link within a few minutes.
