Home / Returns & Exchanges

Our Promise

We want you to love your purchase. If you're not completely satisfied, we offer easy returns and exchanges within 30 days of delivery.

Return Policy

We accept returns on full-price and sale items within 30 days of delivery. Items must be in their original condition with all tags attached.

  • Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in original condition
  • All tags must be attached
  • Original packaging should be included when possible
  • Final sale items and intimate apparel cannot be returned
  • Earrings and jewelry are non-returnable for hygiene reasons
  • Gift cards are non-refundable

Exchange Process

Need a different size or color? We're happy to exchange your item. Here's how:

1

Request Exchange

Log in to My Account and select the order you'd like to exchange, or contact us on WhatsApp.

2

Ship Back

We'll provide a prepaid shipping label. Pack the item securely and drop it off at your nearest courier location.

3

Quality Check

Once we receive your return, we'll inspect it within 1-2 business days.

4

Exchange Sent

Your new item will be shipped within 1-2 business days after quality check is complete.

Non-Returnable Items

  • Earrings and pierceable jewelry
  • Intimate apparel and swimwear
  • Items marked as "Final Sale"
  • Personalized or monogrammed items
  • Gift cards

Damaged or Defective Items

If you receive a damaged or defective item, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the damage. We'll arrange a free return and send a replacement or full refund.
