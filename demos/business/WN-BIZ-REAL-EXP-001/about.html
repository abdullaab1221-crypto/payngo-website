About PrimeNest Properties

Building trust, one property at a time

Our Story

Founded in 2015 by Ahmad Khan, PrimeNest Properties began as a small real estate consultancy in DHA Phase 5. With a vision to transform the real estate experience in Pakistan, we have grown into one of the most trusted agencies in the country.

Over the past decade, we have helped over 1,200 families find their dream homes and assisted investors in building profitable real estate portfolios. Our success is built on integrity, expertise, and an unwavering commitment to client satisfaction.

We specialize in premium residential and commercial properties across Lahore's most desirable neighborhoods, including DHA, Gulberg, Cantonment, and Model Town.
