Mortgage Tips

💡 Save for a Larger Down Payment

A higher down payment reduces your monthly burden and total interest paid over the loan term.

📊 Compare Interest Rates

Even a 0.5% difference in interest rate can save you lakhs over the life of your loan.

⏱️ Shorter Term Saves Money

Opting for a 15-year term instead of 20 can significantly reduce total interest paid.

🏠 Consider Additional Costs

Factor in property tax, insurance, and maintenance costs when budgeting for your new home.

📋 Check Your Credit Score

A good credit score helps you secure better interest rates from banks and financial institutions.

🤝 Consult an Expert

Our mortgage advisors can help you find the best financing options tailored to your needs.
