About Velvet Studio

The story behind Gulberg's premier hair salon

Our Story

Velvet Studio was founded in 2018 by Ayesha Khan, a passionate hairstylist with over 15 years of experience in the beauty industry. What started as a small studio with two chairs has grown into one of Gulberg's most prestigious salons.

Our philosophy is simple: every client deserves a personalized experience. We take the time to understand your lifestyle, face shape, and personal style before recommending any service. There are no cookie-cutter looks here — everything is tailored to you.

We invest heavily in ongoing training for our team, ensuring they stay ahead of global trends. Our stylists regularly attend workshops in Dubai, London, and Istanbul to bring the latest techniques to Lahore.

We exclusively use premium products from L'Oréal Professional, Wella, and Olaplex, ensuring your hair receives the best care possible while delivering results that last.
