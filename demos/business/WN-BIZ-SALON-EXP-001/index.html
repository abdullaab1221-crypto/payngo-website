Welcome to Velvet Studio

Your sanctuary for premium hair and beauty services in Lahore

At Velvet Studio, we believe every client deserves a luxurious, personalized experience. Founded in 2018 by Ayesha Khan, our salon has become Gulberg's most sought-after destination for those who appreciate the finer things in life.

Our team of expert stylists brings international training from London, Dubai, and Istanbul to every appointment. We use only premium products from L'Oréal Professional, Wella, and Olaplex to ensure your hair receives the best care possible.
