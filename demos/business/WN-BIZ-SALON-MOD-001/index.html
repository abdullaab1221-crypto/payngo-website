Welcome to Velvet Studio

Your sanctuary for premium hair and beauty services in Lahore

At Velvet Studio, we believe every client deserves a luxurious experience. Founded in 2018, our salon has become Gulberg's most sought-after destination for those who appreciate the finer things in life.

Our team of expert stylists brings international training and years of experience to every appointment. We use only premium products from top brands like L'Oréal Professional, Wella, and Olaplex to ensure your hair receives the best care possible.

From precision cuts to vibrant color transformations, we offer a comprehensive range of services tailored to your individual style and preferences.
